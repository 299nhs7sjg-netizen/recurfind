    FAQ

    Is my bank data uploaded?

    No. The file is processed by code running in your browser tab. There is no server receiving it. You can turn off your internet after the page loads and it still works. The only network request is the license check when you enter a key.

    Which files work?

    CSV exports from Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citi, Amex, Discover and most other US banks, plus OFX and QFX files. Look for "Download transactions" on your bank's website. Three or more months gives the best results.

    How does it decide something is a subscription?

    It groups charges by merchant, keeps those with a similar amount, and checks whether they repeat about every week, two weeks, month, quarter or year. Well-known services need two matching charges; other merchants need three.
